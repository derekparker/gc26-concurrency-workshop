sync.Mutex

Protects a critical section of any size. Reach for it first; optimize only when a profile says to.

reads dominate

sync.RWMutex

Many readers, rare writers. Only pays off when reads genuinely dominate — heavier than a Mutex under write load.

one word, one op

sync/atomic

Counters and flags. Fast, and easy to misuse for anything needing two operations to stay consistent — see exercise 3.

Prefer the typed wrappers (atomic.Int64) over atomic.AddInt64(&x, 1): they don’t allow non-atomic access, and they carry their own 64-bit alignment — so they sidestep the alignment trap that makes atomic.AddInt64 panic on 32-bit builds. go fix’s atomictypes converts them for you in Go 1.27.
